# Recommended Conference and Journal Shortlist

This document provides a curated list of potential publication venues for the research on **Dynamical Low-Rank Approximation (DLRA) with Structure-Preserving splits for incompressible Navier–Stokes equations**, validated on high-Reynolds-number, forced, turbulent dynamics.

**NOTICE:** *Deadlines are based on projected estimates and historical patterns as of Sep 25, 2026. Final verification against official Call for Papers (CfP) is required prior to submission.*

## Submission Strategy

The strategy follows a dual-track approach:
1.  **Machine Learning Track (Algorithmic Novelty):** Focus on venues that value the mathematical foundations of low-rank approximation and its application to scientific computing (e.g., ICML, NeurIPS). This frames the work as a significant contribution to ML-for-Science.
2.  **Scientific Computing & Physics Track (Numerical Rigor):** Target journals and conferences that emphasize numerical stability, divergence-freeness, and physical validation in fluid dynamics (e.g., JCP, SIAM SISC).

## Detailed Venue Information

### Machine Learning & SciML Track

#### **ICML (International Conference on Machine Learning)**
- **Target Period:** Late January / Early February 2027.
- **Scope:** General machine learning, with a strong interest in "Machine Learning for Science".
- **Relevance:** **Primary Target**. High impact if the contribution is framed as a novel low-rank integration/approximation method for PDEs and validated on turbulence.

#### **NeurIPS (Neural Information Processing Systems)**
- **Target Period:** Mid-May 2027.
- **Scope:** High-impact machine learning research across all subfields.
- **Relevance:** Secondary major target for high-impact ML framing.

#### **AISTATS (Artificial Intelligence and Statistics)**
- **Target Period:** Extremely imminent (End of September / Early October 2026).
- **Scope:** Intersection of statistics and machine learning theory.
- **Note:** Requires immediate mobilization if chosen as a "sprint" submission target.

### Scientific Computing & Physics Track

#### **SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing (SISC)**
- **Submission Type:** Rolling.
- **Focus:** Rigorous numerical analysis, stability, and algorithm development.
- **Relevance:** Excellent for a "structure-preserving" mathematical contribution with strong error analysis and theoretical grounding.

#### **Journal of Computational Physics (JCP)**
- **Submission Type:** Rolling.
- **Focus:** Numerical methods for physical problems and fluid dynamics.
- **Relevance:** Ideal venue to showcase the turbulence validation results and exact divergence-freeness in a physics context.

## Final Recommendation to Reviewer

Based on current progress and research readiness:
1.  **Primary Focus:** Target **ICML 2027**. This allows sufficient time for rigorous implementation, full-grid reference benchmarking, and high-quality writing without the extreme pressure of an imminent deadline.
2.  **Secondary/Immediate Option:** **AISTATS 2027** could be considered only if a draft is ready within the next week (due to the Oct 6 deadline). Given our current state, this may pose a risk to quality.
3.  **Long-term Goal:** Maintain high-quality journal submissions (**JCP/SISC**) as parallel or follow-up tracks once the conference version is matured.
