\begin{abstract}
Solving the incompressible Navier--Stokes equations at high Reynolds numbers
requires resolving the full velocity field on every time step, even when the
dynamically active degrees of freedom are comparatively few. We propose a
structure-preserving dynamical low-rank approximation (SP-DLRA) for the
two-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations in stream-function
form. The method splits the dynamics into a viscous part, which is integrated
\emph{exactly} along a separable exponential flow that preserves the low-rank
ansatz and keeps the velocity exactly divergence-free to machine precision,
and a nonlinear part, which is advanced by a second-order projected integrator.
The rank is adapted online by incremental singular value decomposition. We
validate the method on forced 2D turbulent dynamics at Reynolds numbers
$100$, $1000$, and $5000$ against a full-grid spectral reference and a static
POD baseline, tracking rank growth, accuracy, and cost.

The method is restricted to two dimensions, periodic domains, and single-mode
forcing; rank adaptation combines indicator-driven growth with tolerance-based
decay, and at the tested Reynolds numbers
the per-step cost is comparable to, and in regimes slower than, a full-grid
spectral solver, which we report alongside the benefits.
\end{abstract}
